ABOUT THE PROJECT

Mapping the creative memory of India.

Indian Art History Interactive Map is a lightweight digital atlas designed to make India's artistic heritage easier to explore geographically.

From rock paintings and Buddhist murals to miniature traditions and contemporary art, the map connects places with the artistic movements that emerged there.
